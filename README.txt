IPHONE SETUP
1. On your iPhone, use Safari.
2. The easiest free route is to upload this folder to a static host such as Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages.
3. Open the resulting HTTPS page in Safari.
4. Tap Share -> Add to Home Screen.
5. Your checklist and weigh-ins are stored locally on your iPhone.

WHOOP PERSONALISATION
The app uses your exported WHOOP history: average recovery ~68%, HRV ~70 ms, RHR ~53 bpm, sleep performance ~79%, and typical sleep ~7h49m. Recent data also shows some higher-strain days and variable sleep. The app therefore uses recovery-based intensity rules rather than forcing hard training every day.

Important: WHOOP calorie burn is not treated as a permission to eat extra calories. Use weight trend to calibrate intake.
