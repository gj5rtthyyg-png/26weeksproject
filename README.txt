IPHONE WEB APP

Upload index.html + manifest.json to an HTTPS static host.
Open in Safari -> Share -> Add to Home Screen.

SAVED DAILY DATA
- WHOOP Recovery, HRV, RHR, sleep performance, sleep duration, sleep debt, strain, WHOOP calories
- Weight and optional waist
- Food ingredients with grams eaten, calories/100g and protein/100g
- Calculated calories and protein for each ingredient
- Water cups
- Daily checklist

FOOD TRACKER
For each ingredient, enter grams eaten plus calories and protein per 100 g.
The site calculates:
calories = grams × kcal/100 g ÷ 100
protein = grams × protein/100 g ÷ 100
Quick buttons are included for common foods. Values are examples; use the nutrition label for packaged foods for accuracy.

PROGRESS TAB
Shows the daily history and all saved inputs.
