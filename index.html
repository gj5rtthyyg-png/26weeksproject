WHOOP intensity rule

  • Green / high recovery: do planned session; normal loads.
  • Yellow / middling recovery: keep the session but reduce volume ~20% or choose Zone 2.
  • Red / poor recovery: walk + mobility; skip HIIT and heavy lifting. Resume when recovery improves.

This is a coaching heuristic, not a medical rule. Your own baseline and symptoms matter more than a single score.

Adjustment rule

If your 14–21 day trend is falling ~0.4–0.8 kg/week, keep the plan. If adherence is high but there is no meaningful loss for 2–3 weeks, reduce by ~100–150 kcal/day or add ~1,500–2,000 steps/day. If loss is consistently >0.9 kg/week with poor energy or training, increase intake slightly.
